The small print, in plain words

Why {{ radius }} miles?

It keeps the night realistic. A cab across the city kills the mood and the budget, so any spot further than {{ radius }} miles from the crew's centre point is simply refused.

Where is the centre?

It is the average of everyone who has shared a location, and it moves as people check in. The map draws it, so you always see the circle you are working inside.

Do I need an account?

No. Your spot in the crew is remembered on your device, so keep the link handy and you can come back to the same night.

Can we change our minds?

Always. Votes can be undone, a locked in spot can be reopened, and new suggestions can land right up to the last minute.
